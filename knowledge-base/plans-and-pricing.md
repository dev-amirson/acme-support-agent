# Plans and Pricing

## Plan Comparison

Acme Cloud offers three plans. Prices are per workspace.

| | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $49/month or $490/year | Custom annual contract, from $2,000/month |
| Projects | 1 | 20 | Unlimited |
| Storage | 2 GB | 100 GB | 1 TB included |
| API limit | 60 requests/minute | 600 requests/minute | 3,000 requests/minute |
| Support | Community forum | Email and in-app chat | Email, chat, phone, shared Slack channel |
| Uptime | — | 99.9% target, no service credits | 99.95% SLA with service credits |

## Free Plan

The Free plan is for trying Acme Cloud and for small personal projects.

- Price: $0.
- 1 project and 2 GB of storage.
- API limit of 60 requests/minute.
- Support is through the community forum only. Billing issues can be emailed to the billing team.
- There are no paid charges on the Free plan.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan is for professional developers and small teams.

- Price: $49/month per workspace, or $490/year when billed annually. Annual billing gives you 2 months free compared with paying monthly.
- 20 projects and 100 GB of storage.
- API limit of 600 requests/minute.
- Up to 10 seats at $12/seat/month.
- Email and in-app chat support.
- 99.9% uptime target. The Pro plan does not include service credits.
- Pro subscriptions can be cancelled anytime, effective at the end of the billing period, with no cancellation fee.

## Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan is for organizations that need scale, security controls, and contractual commitments.

- Custom annual contract starting at $2,000/month.
- Unlimited projects and 1 TB of storage included.
- API limit of 3,000 requests/minute, with higher limits available on request through your Customer Success Manager (CSM).
- SSO/SAML and SCIM user provisioning.
- Audit logs, retained for 1 year.
- A dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM).
- 99.95% uptime SLA with service credits.
- Payment by card, ACH, or invoice with net-30 terms.
- Seats are set by the Enterprise contract.

To discuss an Enterprise contract, contact the Acme Cloud sales team through the website or ask support to connect you.

## Upgrading and Downgrading

### Upgrades

- Upgrades take effect immediately.
- Upgrades are prorated: you pay only for the remaining part of the current billing cycle at the new price.
- You can upgrade from Free to Pro, or switch Pro from monthly to annual billing, in Settings → Billing.
- Moving to Enterprise requires a signed Enterprise contract.

### Downgrades

- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. You keep your current plan's features until then.
- Before a downgrade takes effect, bring your usage within the new plan's limits. For example, a downgrade from Pro to Free requires reducing to 1 project and 2 GB of storage.
- Plan changes are made by the customer in Settings → Billing. Support cannot change a plan on your behalf.

## Choosing the Right Plan

- Choose Free if you need 1 project and are comfortable with community forum support.
- Choose Pro if you need up to 20 projects, up to 10 seats, email and in-app chat support, or more than 60 requests/minute.
- Choose Enterprise if you need SSO/SAML, SCIM, audit logs, a dedicated CSM, a contractual uptime SLA with service credits, invoice billing, or more than 20 projects.
