# Refund Policy

## General Refund Rules

These rules apply to every Acme Cloud refund, regardless of plan:

- Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Funds arrive within 5–10 business days after the refund is issued, depending on the customer's bank or card issuer.
- Only one refund can be issued per invoice. If part of an invoice was already refunded, no further refund can be made against that same invoice.
- A refund can never exceed the amount of the invoice it is issued against.
- Every refund is recorded with one of these reasons: duplicate charge, service issue, accidental purchase, billing error, or other.
- Refund eligibility also depends on the account's status (active, past_due, cancelled, or an expired Enterprise contract). See "Account Status and Refund Eligibility" below.

## Free Plan Refunds
<!-- tiers: free -->

The Free plan costs $0 and has no paid charges, so on the Free plan there is normally nothing to refund.

- If a Free plan account shows a charge, it was made in error. This is treated as a billing error, not a standard refund request.
- Billing errors on Free accounts are handled by the billing team. The support assistant creates an escalation ticket with the reason "billing error" and the billing team follows up.
- Free plan users can email billing issues even though general Free plan support is through the community forum.

## Pro Plan Refunds
<!-- tiers: pro -->

On the Pro plan ($49/month or $490/year per workspace), subscription charges can be refunded in full within 14 days of the charge date. This applies to both monthly and annual subscription charges.

### Pro Approval Limits

- The support agent may approve Pro plan refunds of up to $100 per invoice. A refund of a monthly $49 charge, for example, can be approved directly.
- Any Pro plan refund above $100 requires billing team approval. A refund of the $490 annual charge, for example, must be escalated.
- To escalate, the support agent creates an escalation ticket for the billing team. Refund and billing approvals are high priority.

### After the 14-Day Window

- On the Pro plan, subscription charges older than 14 days are non-refundable.
- Acme Cloud does not issue partial-month or prorated refunds for unused time on a Pro subscription.
- The exception is a duplicate charge, which is refundable for 90 days (see "Duplicate Charges").
- Customers who no longer want Pro can cancel at any time; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Enterprise Plan Refunds
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

On the Enterprise plan, the support agent may approve refunds or credits of up to $500 per invoice when the request is made within 30 days of the charge date.

- Enterprise refund or credit requests above $500 require approval by the customer's account manager.
- Enterprise requests made more than 30 days after the charge date also require account manager approval, regardless of amount.
- Requests that need account manager approval are escalated as high priority.
- Credits are applied to future Enterprise invoices; refunds go back to the original payment method.

### Expired Enterprise Contracts

If an Enterprise contract has expired, any refund requires approval by the account manager. The support agent cannot approve refunds on an expired Enterprise contract, even for small amounts.

## Duplicate Charges

A duplicate charge means the customer was charged twice for the same billing period.

- Duplicate charges are refundable in full within 90 days of the charge date on any paid plan (Pro or Enterprise).
- The plan's approval limit still applies. On Pro, the support agent may approve up to $100 per invoice, and larger amounts need billing team approval. On Enterprise, the support agent may approve up to $500 per invoice, and larger amounts need account manager approval.
- Only the duplicate charge is refunded; the original, legitimate charge for the period stays in place.
- Duplicate-charge refunds are the only refunds available to cancelled accounts.
- Record the refund reason as "duplicate charge".

## Non-Refundable Charges

The following charges are not refundable:

- Usage and overage charges, such as bandwidth and storage overage, are non-refundable unless the charge was a billing error or a duplicate charge.
- Pro plan subscription charges older than 14 days, including requests for partial-month refunds.
- Any amount greater than the invoice total, or any second refund on an invoice that already has a refund.

## Account Status and Refund Eligibility

The account's status is checked before any refund is approved:

| Account status | Refund rule |
| --- | --- |
| active | Standard plan rules apply. |
| past_due | No refunds until the outstanding balance is settled. Route to the billing team. |
| cancelled | Only duplicate-charge refunds. Any other request goes to the billing team. |
| expired Enterprise contract | Any refund requires the account manager. |

- A past_due account cannot receive a refund, even an otherwise eligible one, until the outstanding balance is paid.
- For cancelled accounts, the support agent may still process a duplicate-charge refund within the plan's approval limit.

## How Refunds Are Processed

1. Verify the customer's identity and confirm which workspace and invoice the request is about.
2. Check the account status. If it is past_due, cancelled (and not a duplicate charge), or an expired Enterprise contract, escalate as described above.
3. Confirm the invoice has not already been refunded. Only one refund is allowed per invoice.
4. Check the time window: 14 days for Pro subscription charges, 30 days for Enterprise, 90 days for duplicate charges.
5. Confirm the charge type. Usage and overage charges qualify only if they were a billing error or duplicate.
6. Compare the amount with the approval limit: $100 per invoice on Pro, $500 per invoice on Enterprise. The refund cannot exceed the invoice amount.
7. Record the reason: duplicate charge, service issue, accidental purchase, billing error, or other.
8. Issue the refund, or create an escalation ticket for the billing team (Pro) or account manager (Enterprise). The customer receives the ticket ID.
9. Tell the customer that the refund returns to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
