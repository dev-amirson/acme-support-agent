# Contracts and Cancellation

## Cancellation Overview

- Free plan workspaces have no contract and no charges. A Free workspace can simply stop being used, or be deleted from workspace settings.
- Pro plan subscriptions can be cancelled anytime, effective at the end of the billing period, with no cancellation fee.
- Enterprise plan contracts are annual and auto-renew unless written non-renewal notice is given 60 days before the renewal date.
- Cancelling a plan is not the same as deleting a workspace. Workspace deletion is permanent after the 30-day recovery window.
- Cancelling does not by itself create a refund. Refunds follow the Refund Policy.

## Cancelling a Pro Subscription
<!-- tiers: pro -->

On the Pro plan, you can cancel at any time with no cancellation fee.

1. Go to Settings → Billing.
2. Choose to cancel the Pro subscription, or turn off auto-renew.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

What happens next:

- The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period (the end of the month for monthly billing, or the end of the year for annual billing).
- You keep full Pro features until then. When the billing period ends, the subscription stops and the account status becomes cancelled.
- Pro subscription charges are refundable in full only within 14 days of the charge date. After 14 days there are no partial-month refunds, so cancelling mid-cycle does not refund the unused time.
- A cancelled account can only receive duplicate-charge refunds. Other refund requests go to the billing team.
- If you want to keep using Acme Cloud for free instead, downgrade to the Free plan in Settings → Billing. Downgrades also take effect at the end of the billing cycle, and usage must fit the Free plan limits of 1 project and 2 GB of storage.

## Enterprise Contract Term and Auto-Renewal
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

On the Enterprise plan, contracts are annual and start at $2,000/month.

- Enterprise contracts auto-renew at the end of each annual term.
- To stop an Enterprise contract from renewing, the customer must give written non-renewal notice at least 60 days before the renewal date.
- Notice given less than 60 days before the renewal date does not stop that renewal.
- Send non-renewal notice to your account manager or Customer Success Manager (CSM) in writing. Changing the auto-renew setting alone does not replace the written notice.
- Pricing, seats, and storage beyond the included 1 TB are set in the contract.

## Early Termination of an Enterprise Contract
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

An Enterprise customer who wants to end the contract before the end of the annual term can request early termination.

- The early termination fee is 50% of the remaining contract value, meaning the value of the months left in the current annual term.
- Early termination requests are handled by the account manager, not by the support assistant. Support creates an escalation ticket so the account manager can follow up.
- The alternative to early termination is to give written non-renewal notice at least 60 days before the renewal date and let the contract run to the end of its term.

## When an Enterprise Contract Expires
<!-- tiers: enterprise -->

If an Enterprise contract expires without renewal, the workspace enters a 30-day read-only grace period.

- During the 30-day read-only grace period, users can sign in, view projects, and export data, but cannot create or change resources.
- Use the grace period to export data from Settings → Data in JSON or CSV format.
- To restore full access, sign a renewal or a new contract with the account manager.
- On an expired Enterprise contract, any refund requires approval by the account manager.

## Before You Cancel

- Export any data you want to keep from Settings → Data (JSON or CSV, available on all plans).
- Remove projects and storage above the limits of the plan you are moving to.
- Rotate or remove API keys and disable webhooks you no longer need.
- If you are leaving because of a problem, contact support first. Many issues can be fixed, and a service issue may qualify for a refund under the Refund Policy.
